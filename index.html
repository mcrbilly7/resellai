The business

Door-to-door hangers for companies that want to be seen.

Most ads get swiped past. A hanger stays on the handle until someone comes home. Nossonk LLC is a DFW distribution service built for local businesses, contractors, restaurants, real estate teams, political campaigns, and service brands that need a neighborhood to notice them this week — not next quarter.

We do one thing well: we take your printed hangers and put them on doors across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex. You choose the city and the door count. We walk the route.

  • Targeted neighborhoods, not random zip dumps
  • Straightforward 25¢-per-door pricing
  • Travel fee only when we leave Dallas — and it disappears at 1,000 doors
  • Currently serving the DFW Metroplex only

Why hangers work

Physical marketing in a market that still answers the door.

They cannot be skipped like a feed.

A hanger sits on the handle until it is taken inside. That is more attention than a 1.5-second social impression — and it costs a quarter.
